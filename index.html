XRPL Financial Infrastructure Research

Independent, non-commercial research on the XRP Ledger and digital financial infrastructure.

Research topics include:

  • RLUSD payments and transfers
  • XRPL Batch transactions
  • DEX and AMM liquidity
  • XRP bridge transactions
  • MPT and tokenized assets
  • Public wallet attribution research

Research uses publicly available blockchain data and documentation.

This is an independent research project. It is not affiliated with Ripple or Bithomp.
